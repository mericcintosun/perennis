## Kept

- Diagnosis 5's fourth figure, the reactivity precompile `0x…0100`, is kept as
  data but moved into the dateline sentence rather than deleted, because it is
  the one address a judge can check without opening a wallet.
- `public/brand/logo.png` is kept as the one mark even though its raster was
  drawn against the pre-identity palette, because the one-mark rule needs an
  existing mark and `public/brand/*` is outside the Phase 8 fence lift. Recorded
  in `IDENTITY.md` under Amendments.
