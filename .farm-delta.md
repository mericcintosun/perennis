  skeleton, a clay eyebrow over a hairline, a serif h2 and a numbered dispatch
  list. Two figures and a queue sentence now sit between the second h2 and its
  list, which breaks the repeat where it mattered most, but the numbered rhythm
  itself stays: it is the archetype's own form and replacing it a second time
  would be a redesign of a page Phase 8 landed four days ago.

  palette.** Both are rasters this session cannot write, and
  `app/opengraph-image.png` is on the never touch list outright. The masthead
  mark is the one raster the Phase 9 fence lift covers, and it is the only one
  that appears on the page itself.
